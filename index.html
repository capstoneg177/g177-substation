G177 Substation Design Project

Polytechnic University of Puerto Rico

This project presents the design of a step-down substation that receives approximately 32.8 kV from the utility and steps the voltage down to 480 V to supply the facility loads. The design includes load analysis, equipment selection, civil design, grounding and protection, economic evaluation, and complete technical documentation.

Download Full Report View Project Files

Project Overview

The proposed substation reduces the utility voltage of approximately 32.8 kV to 480 V, three-phase, to supply the required facility loads. The design considers reliability, safety, equipment coordination, civil installation requirements, and overall cost-effectiveness.

The project documentation includes load analysis, transformer and equipment selection, substation blueprint set development, grounding and protection considerations, civil layout requirements, and an economic proposal.
